INFINITE LOOP O.E. is a software company based in Greece. We create our own consumer apps and help other companies turn ideas into reliable, well-designed mobile products.

About us

Infinite Loop was founded by mobile engineers Pantelis Giazitsis and Manolis Zervos.

We focus on native technologies (Swift / SwiftUI and Kotlin / Jetpack Compose), cloud back-ends and privacy-friendly use of AI, with special care for families and children.

What we do

  • Native mobile apps

    iOS and Android apps, from the first idea and design to the App Store and Google Play.

  • Cloud back-ends

    Secure, scalable back-ends on Google Cloud and Firebase, with EU data residency.

  • AI-powered features

    Responsible use of modern AI for text, images and voice, with privacy by design.

Our products

Paramythi Coming 2027

Paramythi (Greek for "fairy tale") turns a child's drawing into a personalised, illustrated bedtime story, narrated in Greek. The child is the hero and the characters of their own drawing come alive on every page. Parents can also record their own voice to read the story.

The app is designed for parents, with strict privacy for children's data: drawing photos are deleted after the story is created, data is stored in the EU and is never used to train AI models.
